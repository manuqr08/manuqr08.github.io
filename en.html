Rock, water and greenery

The landscape

The Sierra de la Utrera is a limestone range whose karst landscape is the result of water acting on the rock.

Within the rock lies the Canuto de la Utrera, a narrow, leafy stretch where the vegetation contrasts with the bare stone around it. Nearby are the Baños de la Hedionda, a site linked to water that gives its name to several of the routes.

The routes are about taking in this landscape slowly: how the rock changes, where the water runs and which plants make use of every corner.

Geology, water, plants and wildlife

The Sierra de la Utrera

A limestone massif shaped by water, with gorges, a sulphurous spring and birds that nest on its cliffs. This is what the sources we consulted say.

Geology

The range is made mainly of nodular and oolitic limestones formed in the Jurassic. It is a tectonic window: an outcrop surrounded by geological units of the Internal Betics.

Water dissolving the limestone has created a maze of karst relief that Casares Town Council compares to the Torcal de Antequera.

Sources: Malagapedia: Canuto de Utrera (Spanish) (opens in a new tab) · Casares Town Council: Nature (Spanish) (opens in a new tab)

Water

The range is cut by three narrow valleys running parallel to the coast, known locally as canutos. The Molinos and Albarrán streams join to form the Manilva river.

The Baños de la Hedionda (literally “the smelly baths”) take their name from the smell of their sulphurous water. According to Casares Town Council, the spring's channelling is Roman in origin, was rebuilt in the Islamic period and keeps works from the 17th to the 20th century.

Sources: Malagapedia: Canuto de Utrera (Spanish) (opens in a new tab) · Casares Town Council: Hedionda Baths (opens in a new tab)

Plants

The range has a coastal juniper woodland on limestone, described as the only one of its kind known, and natural carob woodland, which is scarce in Andalusia and on the Mediterranean coast.

Sources: Malagapedia: Canuto de Utrera (Spanish) (opens in a new tab)

Wildlife

Bonelli's eagle, one of the most threatened species in the Mediterranean, lives on the rock walls, along with other cliff birds such as the Egyptian vulture, eagle owl, crag martin and black wheatear.

Otters and kingfishers are recorded on the river, and bats such as horseshoe bats and Schreiber's bat in the caves.

Sources: Casares Town Council: Nature (Spanish) (opens in a new tab) · Malagapedia: Canuto de Utrera (Spanish) (opens in a new tab)

Looking after the range

  • Stay on the path and don't cut across private land.
  • Keep your distance and stay quiet near the cliffs where birds nest.
  • Take all your rubbish home.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How difficult are the routes?

We use the grading of the Wikiloc tracks. The difficult route includes rocky ground, scrambles and sections that need care.

  • Canuto de la Utrera and Baños de la Hedionda: Easy · 3.63 km · 163 m
  • Baños, Poza de la Paloma, Torcal and Canuto: Difficult · 14.11 km · 476 m
  • Baños de la Hedionda, Molino Cancón and Canuto de la Utrera: Moderate · 9.74 km · 352 m
How long does each route take?

These are the times recorded on Wikiloc by whoever logged each track. The length of a guided outing has not been set yet and may be different.

  • Canuto de la Utrera and Baños de la Hedionda: moving time (recorded) 1 h 13 min · total time (recorded) 2 h 28 min
  • Baños, Poza de la Paloma, Torcal and Canuto: moving time (recorded) 5 h 15 min · total time (recorded) 7 h 51 min
  • Baños de la Hedionda, Molino Cancón and Canuto de la Utrera: moving time (recorded) 2 h 23 min · total time (recorded) 3 h 24 min
When is the best time to go?

The project has not set seasons yet. Open dates will appear in the calendar, and the weather forecast and the state of the path are checked before every outing.

What should I bring?

The kit list will be confirmed for each activity. As for any walk in the hills: footwear with good grip, water, sun protection and clothing suited to the forecast. On the difficult route it helps to keep your hands free for the scrambles.

Can I go into or bathe in the Baños de la Hedionda?

It is not part of the proposed routes, because access conditions have not been verified.

How much does it cost and how do I book?

Prices have not been set yet. Once places open, you will be able to request a date from the calendar; sending a request does not confirm a place.
